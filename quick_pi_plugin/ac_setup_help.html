WayriCAD Quick PI AC / SMPS setup

  1. Start in Quick loop. Enter conductor length, DC rail voltage, DC current and fixed copper temperature.
  2. Choose stacked, side-by-side or split return. Enter copper width/thickness, surface gap, ripple frequency in kHz and ripple RMS current in A. The preview shows explicit geometry and current direction, before any solve.
  3. Click Run AC / ripple losses. Run applies the visible quick recipe. There is no separate Apply step to remember.
  4. For imported models, multiple conductors or an SMPS spectrum, choose Custom loop. The conductor/harmonic tables and solver settings expand. Custom Run preserves those tables. Load study and Save inputs are available at the top.
  5. Review Heating & drop: total copper heat, ripple heat, AC loop RMS voltage drop and DC-plus-ripple RMS current. Select a large plot with Inspect. A single frequency produces a discrete comparison, not a sweep. Tables use kHz, mΩ and mW.
  6. Select a harmonic/conductor row to highlight it in the plot. Drag the divider to resize plots and tables. The Solver checks page explains refinement and applicability; incomplete checks remain explicit. Export HTML/JSON for all three report plots and full SI-unit evidence.

Reading the numbers

QuantityMeaning
Total copper heatDC copper Joule loss plus the supplied ripple harmonics, in watts. This is heating power, not temperature.
Ripple heatingThe AC contribution only; it is already included in total heat.
AC loop dropRMS copper-loop voltage from the supplied harmonics, including resistive and reactive effects.
DC + ripple RMSThe reference-loop current with its mean and supplied distinct-frequency RMS components combined. Per-conductor current multipliers still apply.

Quick setup takes RMS ripple. For measured peak-to-peak triangular ripple or pulsed SMPS current, use the custom waveform builder with its explicitly labelled inputs. A finite harmonic band does not include omitted edges or simulate the converter.

Model

The AC solver is a current-driven magnetoquasistatic long-straight cross-section model with an explicit balanced return. It resolves skin/proximity redistribution and Joule copper loss. Signed conductor-current multipliers must sum to zero. Zero-current conductors can represent unpowered shields.

For each harmonic, copper loss is the volume integral of |J_RMS|²/σ using RMS quantities; loop impedance and conductor losses follow the existing field solution. DC and distinct harmonic losses add at the declared fixed copper temperature. A quick single-harmonic recipe is not a complete switching waveform.

Limits

Quick layouts replace the conductor/harmonic tables when applied. They do not infer board routes, via transitions, component behavior or return paths. The split-return current fractions are assumptions, not predicted current sharing. No regulator dynamics, converter-device loss, 3D routing field, high-voltage insulation or thermal equilibrium is established. Increase bandwidth and refine mesh/boundary settings as needed; unresolved checks stay explicit.
