# Acknowledgements and AI-development disclosure

WayriCAD is maintained by [Wayri (Yawar)](https://github.com/wayri). We thank contributors, users who report reproducible problems, and the communities whose software and research support the project.

## AI-assisted development

Development has made substantial use of AI coding assistants and large language models (LLMs), including **OpenAI Codex**. Assistance has included designing and implementing features, refactoring, debugging, writing and running tests, reviewing code, drafting documentation, researching numerical methods, and preparing releases. Some work has used multiple collaborating AI agents.

This disclosure does not mean that every file was generated by AI or that every change received independent human review. Maintainers remain responsible for project direction, accepting contributions and release decisions. AI assistance is not evidence of correctness, originality, license clearance or engineering certification. Generated code, citations and explanations can be incomplete or wrong.

We report checks actually performed, distinguish automated tests from native application checks, and retain unsupported or approximate states in engineering results. Tests written or executed with AI assistance are not, by themselves, independent validation. Analytical references, convergence checks and physical measurements have different scopes; agreement with a simplified reference does not establish real-world accuracy for an arbitrary design. See [validation and limitations](docs/audits/RELEASE_3.2_VALIDATION.md), [compatibility](docs/COMPATIBILITY.md) and the individual plugin guides.

This statement concerns development tooling. It does not claim that plugin calculations are performed by an LLM. Runtime dependencies, local operation and setup requirements are described in the [installation guide](docs/INSTALLATION.md).
