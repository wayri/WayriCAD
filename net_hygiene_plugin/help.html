KiWay Net Hygiene

Scans the open PCB for common connectivity and naming problems.

Use

  1. Open the board and launch the plugin.
  2. Click Scan.
  3. Review duplicate references, unconnected pads, single-pad nets, and suspicious names.
  4. Export the issue list to CSV for review or CI processing.

Common issues

  • False single-pad net: check whether the net is intentionally reserved for a test point or future option.
  • Unconnected pad: connect it, mark it intentionally no-connect in the schematic, or document the exception.
  • Duplicate reference: annotate the schematic and update the PCB.
