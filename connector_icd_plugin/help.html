KiWay Connector ICD Builder

Builds a connector pin and net table from the open PCB.

Use

  1. Open the board and launch the plugin.
  2. Review detected references beginning with J, P, or CN, plus values containing CONN or HEADER.
  3. Click Export CSV and use the output as an ICD or harness review table.

Common issues

  • Connector missing: rename the reference conventionally or include CONN in the footprint value.
  • Blank net: update the PCB from the schematic and confirm the pad is connected.
  • Incorrect connector classification: review the CSV before using it as a controlled ICD.
